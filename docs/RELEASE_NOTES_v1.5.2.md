## Sbobina 1.5.2 (build 9)

Bug-fix release.

### What's fixed
- **Copy and Share are always reachable.** With a very long transcription the buttons were pushed off screen, so you could not copy or share the text (seen when opening a result from the "Transcription ready" notification). The text now scrolls in its own area and the buttons stay in view. Thanks to the reporter of issue #1.

Full list: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/giulianoprati/sbobina/blob/master/CHANGELOG.md)

### Install
Android 10+, arm64. Download `Sbobina.apk`, allow "Install unknown apps", open it and download the model once (Wi-Fi recommended). Updates over the previous version keep your model and history.
